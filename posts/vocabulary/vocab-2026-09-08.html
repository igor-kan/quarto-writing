1. Expressions and Phrasal Verbs

1. As-yet

  • Part of Speech: Adverb
  • Definition: Up until the present time; not yet (used to describe something that has not happened or is not known so far, but is expected to change).
  • Examples: “An as-yet unidentified suspect was seen leaving the building.” “The company is working on an as-yet unannounced project.”
  • Synonyms: Hitherto, thus far, up to now.

2. Pertains to

  • Part of Speech: Phrasal Verb
  • Definition: To be connected with, related to, or applicable to something.
  • Examples: “Please submit all documents that pertain to the ongoing investigation.”
  • Synonyms: Relates to, applies to, concerns, regards.

2. Nouns and Everyday Terminology

3. Roller coaster

  • Part of Speech: Noun (also used figuratively)
  • Definition: Literally, an amusement park attraction consisting of a light railway track with many tight turns and steep slopes. Figuratively, a situation characterized by wild and unpredictable changes or emotional highs and lows.
  • Examples:
    • Literal: “The wooden roller coaster is the oldest in the park.”
    • Figurative: “Her first year of college was an emotional roller coaster.”

4. Assertion

  • Part of Speech: Noun
  • Definition: A confident and forceful statement of fact or belief.
  • Examples: “The scientist’s assertion that the earth was warming was backed by decades of data.” “I don’t agree with his assertion that we are powerless.”
  • Synonyms: Declaration, contention, statement, claim.

5. Assortment

  • Part of Speech: Noun
  • Definition: A miscellaneous collection of things or people.
  • Examples: “The gift basket contained a wide assortment of luxury chocolates.”
  • Synonyms: Variety, mixture, array, collection, medley.
